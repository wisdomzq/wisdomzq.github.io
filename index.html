Zequn Li / 李泽群

Incoming Ph.D. student · Tsinghua University

I am an undergraduate in Computer Science and Technology at Beijing Institute of Technology. I have been admitted to the direct Ph.D. program in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, where I will be advised by Prof. Jiannan Wang.

My research interests lie at the intersection of data systems and large language models, with related work in natural language processing and computer vision. I enjoy building efficient, practical AI systems.

My recent work explores how indexing, adaptive query execution, and data selection can make LLM-powered document analysis more efficient and accessible.
