Hello, I am

Zequn Li 李泽群

I am an undergraduate student in the Elite Program of Computer Science and Technology at Beijing Institute of Technology. My interests lie at the intersection of data systems, large language models, natural language processing, and computer vision.

I enjoy building efficient, practical AI systems. My recent work studies how indexing, adaptive execution, and data-centric learning can reduce the cost of LLM-powered document analysis while preserving accuracy.
