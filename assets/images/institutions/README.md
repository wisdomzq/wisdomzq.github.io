# University emblems

These official emblems identify the institutions in the Education section.
They remain the property of their respective universities and do not imply
endorsement of this personal website.

- `bit-emblem.jpg`: Beijing Institute of Technology,
  [official explanation of the university emblem](https://www.bit.edu.cn/gbxxgk/dqgz_sjb/a173964.htm).
  [Original image](https://www.bit.edu.cn/images/2026-10/bit_xiaohui.jpg).
- `tsinghua-emblem.jpg`: Tsinghua University,
  [official visual-identity guide: university emblem](https://vi.tsinghua.edu.cn/gk/xxbz/xh.htm).
  [Original image](https://vi.tsinghua.edu.cn/__local/8/1C/21/2F02A66E31BF084FE340463BB47_B9A95F73_185BC.jpg).

The downloaded files are unchanged. CSS trims only the extra white side margins
of the Tsinghua presentation image; the emblem is not recolored, distorted, or
redrawn. Both emblems are shown on a plain white background.
